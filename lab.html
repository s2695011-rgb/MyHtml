Research Introduction: Image Denoising

Our laboratory researches advanced image denoising systems. When traditional filters remove random noise from a photo, they unfortunately blur out sharp edges and important details. To solve this bottleneck, we develop autonomous mathematical frameworks that can automatically measure noise intensity and wash it away while perfectly preserving the image's sharp contours and details. This technology is highly practical and widely used in smartphone night photography and satellite systems.
